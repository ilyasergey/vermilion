# 2026-07-14 — F3: range for-loops; all three case studies verified

Landed the third and largest case-study-driven feature: `for i in lo..hi`
loops, by sound specialization of Verus's `VerusForLoopWrapper<Range<_>>`
desugaring (commit 4b5931f; design record 784ce7f).

- **Semantics pinned to vstd**: `peek`'s inclusive guard puts the exit
  invariant instance at `hi`; `arbitrary()` is one fixed unknown, so entry
  and exit share a single fresh `arb` symbol (empty-range soundness: the
  exit assumption is exactly the entry check). Iterations assume
  `lo ≤ i < hi`; the back edge re-checks at `i+1` with measure `hi − i`.
- **Recognizer fails closed** on everything non-canonical: non-range
  iterators, `for x in y: e`, wrapper-referencing invariants/bodies, user
  `break`/`continue`, user loop-ensures, bodies assigning the loop variable
  or the bounds. Dropped wrapper bookkeeping is enumerated in docs/trust.md;
  the exhausted arm's `has_resolved` borrow expiries relocate to the
  continuation. No IR growth; the Lean (LOOP) rule is untouched (notes added
  to docs/ir.md and docs/vcgen.md; DESIGN.md implemented list updated).
- **Tests**: `examples/m2-forloop` (12 auto + 1 interactive); differential
  c117–c120 including both peek-semantics pins (`2..2` passes both
  verifiers, reversed bounds fail both) — 119/119 verdict and 57/57 span
  parity; case studies now run in the suite (`scripts/run_suite.sh`).
- **Case study `vec-reverse` verified end to end**: 20 obligations, 14
  automatic + 6 interactive twin proofs (double-update swap permutation;
  the final permutation ensures also proven interactively for speed at the
  user's request). All three `vectors.rs` case studies — binary-search,
  vec-uninterp, vec-reverse — are now verified.
